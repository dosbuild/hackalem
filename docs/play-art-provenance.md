# Original artwork

Aya's six-expression guide atlas was generated for Alem with ImageGen. It is
original project artwork, not an image of a real person. Its transparent sprite
sheet uses three columns and two rows: idle, thought and attention on the first
row; explanation, success and error on the second. Lossless WebP assets retain
the same artwork. The guide is decorative; controls remain usable without it.

The city mark, resident SVG portraits, procedural buildings and project models,
native people, materials and ornaments were created for Alem. Resident portraits
and world characters share the fictional identity catalogue. Project preview
images show the game's own meshes, materials and camera.

No reference-game artwork, commercial imagery or research photographs are
redistributed. Bundled fonts and geographic data retain their separate licenses.
