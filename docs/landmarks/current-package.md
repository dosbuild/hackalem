# Astana landmark sources

Alem uses original procedural, stylized architectural models placed on attributed
OpenStreetMap geography. Research photographs are not shipped as textures or
source assets. Headline dimensions guide silhouettes; facade subdivisions,
secondary structures, ornament and some heights are authored estimates, not
engineering measurements.

Architectural references include:

- [Lindner: The Sphere, EXPO 2017](https://www.lindner-group.com/en/references/the-sphere-expo-2017).
- [A-VM: Bayterek](https://a-vm.kz/en/objects/bayterek/).
- [Khan Shatyr](https://site.khanshatyr.com/en/about).
- [HKR Architects: Abu Dhabi Plaza](https://hkrarchitects.com/journals/abu-dhabi-plaza/).
- [Hill International: Grand Astana Mosque](https://www.hillintl.com/project/grand-astana-mosque/).
- [Adrian Smith + Gordon Gill: EXPO 2017](https://www.smithgill.com/work/expo-2017/), [LDP International](https://www.ldpinternational.com/projects/astanaworldexpo), and [Skymax](https://skymaxinnovations.kz/project/vystavochnyy-kompleks-ekspo-2017-6/) for surrounding pavilion forms.
- [CSCEC: Astana LRT bridge](https://ru.cscec.com/xwzx_ru/gsxw_ru/202309/3716499.html) for the rail crossing; arch rises and member sections remain visual estimates.

Source footprints and replacement IDs are preserved in the landmark geography and
pavilion, grounds and annex extracts. Native models replace explicitly identified
source buildings, not arbitrary nearby geometry. The prepared city retains source
building identity and its separate cartographic height treatment.

The LRT route is an attributed OpenStreetMap extract dated 27 September 2026,
distributed under ODbL 1.0 with its way IDs, coordinates and station records.
It represents the mapped corridor rather than a claim of complete operational
coverage. Train movement and station dwell are illustrative, not a live timetable.
Scenario transport initiatives remain separately labelled educational treatments.

All landmarks share the game's world projection, physical depth and lighting.
They are visual interpretations of a city, not a geographic or legal survey.
