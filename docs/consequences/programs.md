# Astana physical programs

The numerical evaluator and the seventy authoritative placement/encounter records
remain unchanged. `src/world/astana-programs.json` adds derived presentation sites
to each parent. Local purchases use only their district; citywide purchases collect
the same district records across all five districts. Facility and actor counts are
authored illustrations, not calculated outcome quantities.

The landscape program retains twelve complete mapped park/square polygons,
including the requested neighboring squares. It places 1,713 individual groves,
flower gardens, meadow patches, occupied seating places and entrances into usable
space, preserving source buildings, paths, water and vegetation. Pockets stay inside
each zone's data; navigation exposes the two or three complete zones per district.

Healthcare retains each original hub and adds three source-supported neighborhood
satellites. Each has a 24 × 18 m building, accessible approach and waiting court
inside its independently checked 40 × 36 m envelope. Deterministic farthest-point
selection uses inhabited residential clusters and prefers 800 m separation, with
400 m as the minimum fallback. Source street/path approaches are checked through
their endpoint. School kindergarten additions stay within 300 m of their school.

Each district has exactly three actual road junctions with source branches and
individually clear signal positions. The forty-second authored illustration has
opposing-axis green intervals, amber only for the departing axis, clearance and
a pedestrian interval with stopped queues. The baseline uses a slower independent
approach rhythm. Protected school crossings show an approach slowdown and a
pedestrian stop. These animations make no numerical traffic or safety claims.

The other programs retain their purpose-built primary treatments and gain checked
working places, boarding and two-way traffic, school grounds, distributed sports
courts, residential conversions and diminished smoke, planting belts/nurseries,
occupied lamp routes, intake/reply activity, household utility service points,
and emergency dispatch/assistance/return. Existing street fixtures are retained
where clear and moved to a nearby checked street pocket when their full physical
kit would touch source geometry, vegetation or another program. Original encounter
and parent origins do not move.

`projectPresentation` supplies preview/baseline/planned/evaluated evidence.
Baseline omits purchased architecture and retains baseline witnesses under the
same animation clock. Exact score/effect prose still comes from `ScenarioResult`.
`projectStaticMesh` builds completed architecture separately from the bounded
`projectDynamicMesh`; park construction reveals prefixes of cached geometry, so
the entire zone's planting is not rewritten every clock update. Overview outlines
use actual area boundaries and site positions, handing off by zoom 18.
Every new physical site, planting pocket, signal and actor uses city ground at
world z = 0.00004. Facility emitted pads are audited at that same elevation;
source rooftop heights remain intact. Household service cabinets have clear
surface lots; their authored buried lines may pass beneath existing streets and
vegetation and are separately checked against buildings, water and district scope.

Regenerate with `node scripts/consequences/prepare-programs.mjs`. Independently
check with `node scripts/consequences/audit-programs.mjs`; the audit re-reads the
complete prepared city and environment rather than invoking the selector. It
checks individual solids, emitted facility envelopes, source access, residential
support, separation, district scope, mutual traffic release, pedestrian stops and
inter-program overlap. Raw authoring source remains outside the runtime allowlist.

Targeted tests are in `tests/astana-programs.test.js`. The original crossing paint
and preparation geometry remain exact; roadside signs now test actual vetted
positions instead of freezing an obsolete furniture byte hash.
