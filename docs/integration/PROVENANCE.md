# Resident scenarios and sources

The 240 appeals are authored educational scenarios: 48 per district. Seventy
interactive stories cover fourteen initiatives across five districts; 170 further
reading cases have their own circumstances and requests. Names, groups, identifiers
and detailed situations are fictional. These are not private eOtinish submissions
or an imported GovStat database.

Public sources inform themes and procedures:

- [eOtinish](https://eotinish.kz/ru): citizen appeals service.
- [Government explanation of eOtinish](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/pavlodar-transport/press/news/details/942252?lang=ru): purpose and submission procedure.
- [Astana school planning](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/675700?lang=ru): education capacity.
- [Astana healthcare publication](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/1136763?lang=ru): healthcare access and infrastructure.
- [Astana development plan 2021–2025](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/documents/details/574960?lang=ru): transport, education, healthcare, safety, utilities and environment.
- [Astana gasification](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/922284?lang=ru): utility access.
- [Astana greenery and irrigation](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/879062?lang=ru): planting and irrigation maintenance.

These publications support themes, not the truth of any fictional complaint or
current conditions at a specific address. Each scenario exposes its references
and limitations. The supplied five districts are model scopes, and stories do not
change the numerical assumptions. A construction animation illustrates a purchase;
it does not establish that an appeal has been resolved.

The interface and authored stories are in English. See
[sources and licenses](../../ATTRIBUTION.md) for artwork and geographic attribution.
