# Residents and visual provenance

The 240 appeals are authored educational scenarios: 48 per district. Seventy interactive stories cover 14 initiatives × five districts; 170 additional formal reading cases have their own circumstances and requests. Names, groups, scenario identifiers and detailed situations were written for Alem. They are not real private submissions or case numbers. No GovStat database is claimed.

The in-product wording is: «Сценарные обращения по открытым материалам о тематике и работе eOtinish». An initiative's completed animation is not proof that an appeal is resolved. Conversation states distinguish consideration, commitment, calculated change, partial improvement and unresolved concerns.

## Public material checked on 28 September 2026

- [eOtinish](https://eotinish.kz/ru): public service and themes of citizen appeals.
- [Government explanation of eOtinish](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/pavlodar-transport/press/news/details/942252?lang=ru): purpose and submission procedure.
- [Astana school planning](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/675700?lang=ru): public education capacity theme; historical publication, not a current measured shortage for a fictional address.
- [Astana healthcare publication](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/1136763?lang=ru): healthcare access and infrastructure theme.
- [Astana development plan 2021–2025](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/documents/details/574960?lang=ru): education, transport, health, safety, utilities and environmental policy themes; historical plan.
- [Astana gasification](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/922284?lang=ru): utility access theme.
- [Astana greenery and irrigation](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/astana/press/news/details/879062?lang=ru): maintenance of planting and irrigation theme.

These sources support themes and process, not the truth of each authored complaint. Per-appeal links and limitations are shown under «Откуда этот сценарий». All requests and conversations are original writing; no database of private complainants is copied.

## Artwork

- `/living/winter-terrace.png` is the original `mayer/game/assets/astana-winter.png`, generated for the project with ImageGen on 24 September 2026. Its provenance is recorded in `mayer/visual-direction/ASSETS.md`. It is an illustration, not a photograph or exact map.
- `/living/M1-installed.svg` through `M14-installed.svg` adapt the project's own vector geometry from `mayer/game/astana-props.js`. No third-party game/reference images are shipped.
- The people use Alem's original procedural pixel sprite system with authored identities and appearances.
- `/aya/cloud-{idle,attention,thought,explanation,success,error}.png` are the six generated plush-cloud expressions produced with the available ImageGen workflow during this integration and inspected in the actual assistant control.
- The geographic city uses the preserved OSM/Geofabrik source and attribution. Construction is authored procedural geometry. Visual receipts and the eight-quarter model are separate.

The five districts are the supplied scenario's scopes. The numerical model and its educational assumptions remain unchanged by stories, artwork and generated prose.
