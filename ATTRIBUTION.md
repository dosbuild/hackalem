# Alem — sources and licenses

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Geographic database licensed under the
[Open Database License 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Prepared from [Geofabrik Kazakhstan, 2026-09-24](https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/kazakhstan-260924.osm.pbf).
The exact fingerprint, object identifiers, source tags and boundary provenance are
in the manifest and quality report inside the [prepared database](../geography.tar.gz).
The derived database remains ODbL data and is available with this release.

OSM is community-maintained, not a legal survey. Estimated building heights are
marked by their source. The five historical scenario districts are model scopes;
any fallback boundaries are labelled approximate. The planar suspended-city presentation and
exaggerated heights are authored cartography, not survey measurements.

The application uses React and React DOM (MIT) and geometry preparation uses
Earcut (ISC); license texts are published under `/licenses/`. The inactive Cesium
prototype remains in the separate development checkout and is excluded from this
production repository and its history.
No commercial imagery, Google Earth screenshots, reference game art, network-loaded
fonts, map-provider keys or third-party tracking scripts are runtime assets.

Aya’s six-expression illustrated guide atlas was created for Alem with ImageGen
on 3 October 2026. The original and lossless WebP delivery share that artwork.
Resident portraits, matching native people, city brand, project meshes, procedural
materials and catalogue previews are authored for this game. Their source and
provenance remain in `docs/play-art-provenance.md` and the native model builders.
The retired winter illustration and plush-cloud prototype are not runtime assets.

Local Noto Sans/Noto Serif and Shantell Sans use the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Their license files ship under `/licenses/`, including Shantell’s official
attribution and the local UI-axis optimization record. Font source and coverage
are checked without a CDN or network font request.

The residents catalogue contains 240 authored formal scenarios, including 70
interactive stories. Names, circumstances and identifiers are fictional. Public
eOtinish materials and official Astana publications inform their themes; these are
not private submissions or a GovStat dataset. Each appeal includes its source links
and limitations. See `docs/integration/PROVENANCE.md` in the source for the full
source record. The earlier geographic resident cast remains original scenario data.
Native Kazakh language review has not been performed.

The initiative and district data are a supplied educational scenario, not current
statistics or personal predictions. Landmark geometry and source anchors are
documented in `docs/landmarks/`.

The October 2026 landmark package uses original procedural architecture based on
photographic references and independently attributed footprints. Research photos
are not shipped. The LRT extract comes from the existing local OpenStreetMap
snapshot dated 2026-09-27; its source way IDs, route coordinates and station
records are published under ODbL 1.0 at
[`/alem/lrt-route.json`](../alem/lrt-route.json). Train animation is illustrative,
not a live service feed. See `docs/landmarks/current-package.md` in source for
architectural evidence, dimensional estimates and implementation notes.
